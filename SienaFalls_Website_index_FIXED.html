Case Study
Catholic Women’s Media Platform
Newsletter · Substack · National
The situation

A Catholic women’s spirituality newsletter with an established readership needed a growth strategy and revenue model. Content was strong; the business architecture wasn’t.

The work

A full audit of the platform, a close look at who was actually subscribing and why, a revenue SWOT, a content calendar, an email strategy to turn readers into subscribers and subscribers into revenue, and a step-by-step guide to put it all into practice.

The outcome

A clear picture of who was actually reading, a revenue model built around that audience, and a 90-day plan with specific content and milestones.

Case Study
Organizational Consultant, Northern Virginia
Service Business · Local Market · 10+ years
The situation

An established organizational consultant with over a decade of experience was seeing client numbers decline. She had a clear brand identity, but every new client came from her personally reaching out — nothing was bringing clients in on its own.

The work

A discovery call, a review of her social media numbers, a look at how her website was presenting her to visitors, a review of where she was spending time trying to get seen, and a full strategy report on why clients were leaving and how to turn that around.

The outcome

A focused strategy built around two strong referral partnerships — realtors and senior move managers — plus a speaking program designed to turn the people who hear her talk into steady referrals.

Siena Falls started as a web design studio in Northern Virginia. Over time the work evolved from building platforms to advising the women behind them on how to grow what they’d built.

I work with Catholic women entrepreneurs who are past the startup phase and hitting some kind of plateau. My clients aren’t struggling because they’re bad at their work. They’re struggling because strategy is different from execution, and most of us learned execution first.

I do a deep intake on your business, make sense of what I find, and build you a strategy package: honest diagnosis, prioritized recommendations, step-by-step action plan. All delivered in 5–7 business days. The speed is real; it’s the result of using AI as a research and synthesis tool the way a lawyer uses Westlaw or a financial analyst uses Bloomberg. The thinking, the judgment, and the strategy are mine.

Siena Falls is named in honor of St. Catherine of Siena. She was a 14th-century woman who counseled popes and heads of state, dictating letters through several secretaries, all while staying rooted in humility and service. She didn't seek authority; people sought her out because her clarity came from prayer, not ambition. That spirit of humble, faithful service is the founding spirit here.

I’m also connected to My Secret is Mine, a Catholic women’s spirituality newsletter rooted in Edith Stein’s philosophy of feminine genius. The Siena Falls model was shaped by that engagement, and by years of building websites, running analytics, and watching what actually moves the needle for small organizations trying to grow something worth growing.
